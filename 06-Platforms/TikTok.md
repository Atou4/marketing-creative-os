# TikTok

Prioritize native pacing, human presence/demo, fast context, captions, and repeatable series. Do not make polished brand-film aesthetics the default if the idea needs native creator language.

Platform rules change. Recheck current official specs before production when dimensions, limits, or policy compliance matter.
