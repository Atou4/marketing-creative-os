# Instagram

Prioritize visual clarity, Reels/carousels, saves/shares, stories, strong first frame/slide, and mobile-safe text. Adapt captions and CTA to the post; do not over-rely on hashtags.

Platform rules change. Recheck current official specs before production when dimensions, limits, or policy compliance matter.
