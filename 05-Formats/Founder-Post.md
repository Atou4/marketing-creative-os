# Founder / Expert Post

Use when the person has a real point of view or firsthand experience.

Good sources:
- product decisions;
- lessons learned;
- customer patterns;
- behind-the-scenes tradeoffs;
- category opinions;
- launch context.

AI may structure and edit; it should not invent the founder's history, emotions, or dialogue.
