# Story

Stories are good for immediacy and interaction.

Use for:
- behind-the-scenes;
- polls/questions;
- event/live context;
- short demos;
- deadline reminders when real;
- social proof;
- click-through or DM prompts.

Do not simply crop feed posts; simplify and recompose for vertical, fast tapping, and UI-safe zones.
