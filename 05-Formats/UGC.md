# UGC

UGC works when it feels like a credible person communicating a specific experience or demonstration.

## Valid sources
- real creator/customer content with permission;
- founder/team content;
- clearly fictional or AI presenter content that does not impersonate a real customer or invent testimony.

## Common structures
- reaction + demo;
- how-to;
- unboxing / first look;
- problem/solution;
- founder explanation;
- FAQ response;
- greenscreen commentary;
- screen + face split.

Never fabricate customer identity or testimonial experience.
