# Paid Ad Creative

Paid creative begins with an angle slate, not with headline variations.

## Inputs
- audience/awareness;
- offer;
- landing-page promise;
- recent winners/losers;
- comments/reviews;
- brand/compliance;
- placement.

## Testing principle
Change one meaningful dimension when possible: angle, hook, format, proof, offer, visual, or CTA. Log what changed.

Use preflight scoring to remove obvious weak/mismatched executions, not to declare winners before spend.
