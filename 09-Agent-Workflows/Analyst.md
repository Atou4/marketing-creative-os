# Analyst

Analyze by business objective, not vanity metrics alone.

Compare:
- hook/angle;
- format;
- persona;
- awareness stage;
- visual pattern;
- CTA;
- offer;
- distribution context.

Separate signal from noise. A single viral outlier should not rewrite the entire strategy.
