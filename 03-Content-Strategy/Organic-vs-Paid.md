# Organic vs Paid

## Organic can optimize for
- attention;
- saves/shares;
- community response;
- education;
- identity and brand affinity;
- repeatable formats.

## Paid must additionally handle
- audience temperature;
- offer/landing match;
- compliance;
- conversion economics;
- fatigue;
- deliberate testing structure.

Do not assume an organic winner automatically becomes a paid winner, but use organic response as one signal for creative exploration.
