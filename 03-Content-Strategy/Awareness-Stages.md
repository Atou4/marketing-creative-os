# Awareness Stages

## Unaware
Lead with the lived situation, tension, or identity—not the product.

## Problem-aware
Name the problem and reframe why it persists.

## Solution-aware
Teach the category/approach and tradeoffs.

## Product-aware
Show product workflows, comparisons, proof, objections.

## Most aware
Lead with offer, urgency only when real, friction removal, and clear CTA.

Angle, hook, proof, and CTA should match the stage.
