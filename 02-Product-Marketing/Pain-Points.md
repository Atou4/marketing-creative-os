# Pain Points

For every pain, capture:
- situation;
- frequency;
- operational cost;
- emotional tension;
- existing workaround;
- why workaround fails;
- exact customer phrasing;
- strongest proof that the product helps.

Avoid manufacturing pain purely to create dramatic hooks.
