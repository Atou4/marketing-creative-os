# Competitor Research

For each competitor collect:
- positioning;
- common angles;
- recurring formats;
- offers;
- proof style;
- customer objections in public comments/reviews;
- visual codes;
- gaps and overused claims.

Use competitor ads as **market evidence and inspiration**, not copy targets. Never assume a long-running ad is profitable just because it is visible.
