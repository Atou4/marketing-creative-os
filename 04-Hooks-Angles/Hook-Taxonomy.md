# Hook Taxonomy

Hooks earn the next second; they are not the whole strategy.

## Families
- Pain / friction
- Outcome / transformation
- Curiosity gap
- Contrarian / reframe
- Identity / “for people like me”
- Proof / result
- Demonstration
- Story opening
- Mistake / warning
- Question
- News / timely relevance

Avoid treating hook formulas as Mad Libs. Use specific audience language and a real angle underneath.
