# Angle Scoring

Preflight scoring is for prioritization, **not prediction**.

Score 1–5:
- Relevance to a real pain/desire
- Specificity
- Evidence strength
- Novelty vs recent creative
- Product/offer fit
- Format fit
- Brand fit
- Execution feasibility

Do not create a fake “virality probability.” Final validation comes from real audience behavior.
