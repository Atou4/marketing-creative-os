# Repo Ingest — AI Copywriter

Source: https://github.com/mikiarlo3/ai-copywriter  
Evidence: community/open-source skill  
License observed: MIT

## Useful patterns ingested
- Copywriting and “humanizing” should be one workflow, not separate afterthoughts.
- Reader-first questions improve relevance.
- AI-writing tells can be turned into explicit review checks.
- No-fabrication rules matter for stories, dialogue and numbers.

## Adopt
Anti-AI-slop copy review + reader-first tests + no-fabrication rule.
