# Repo Ingest — Microsoft Agentic Marketing

Source: https://github.com/microsoft/Agent-Framework-Samples/tree/main/09.Cases/AgenticMarketingContentGen

## Useful patterns ingested
- Strategy, copy, image and video are specialist stages.
- Research can be a deeper Planner → Researcher → Analyst path.
- Outputs are packaged as structured campaign artifacts rather than scattered chat messages.
- Video planning includes script, scene breakdown, screen text, duration and captions.

## Adopt
Specialized roles + structured Campaign Package + research phase + explicit scene breakdowns.
