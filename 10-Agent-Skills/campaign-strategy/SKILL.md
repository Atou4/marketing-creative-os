# Campaign Strategy

## Mandatory context
Read `01-Brand-Brain/MASTER-CONTEXT.md`, relevant `02-Product-Marketing/` notes, and project context first.

## Truth
Never invent claims, testimonials, data, customer stories, partnerships, or product behavior.

1. Clarify objective and conversion.
2. Pick persona + awareness stage.
3. Gather first-party/external signals.
4. Generate 3–5 evidence-linked angles.
5. Score angles for relevance, evidence, novelty, fit, feasibility.
6. Recommend a creative mix.
7. Define experiment questions and required proof.
8. Stage the plan for human selection before production.
