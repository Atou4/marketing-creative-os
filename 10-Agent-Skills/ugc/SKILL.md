# UGC

## Mandatory context
Read `01-Brand-Brain/MASTER-CONTEXT.md`, relevant `02-Product-Marketing/` notes, and project context first.

## Truth
Never invent claims, testimonials, data, customer stories, partnerships, or product behavior.

Choose source type: real creator/customer with permission, founder/team, or clearly synthetic presenter.

Never make an AI presenter pretend to be a real customer with invented experience.

Output: hook variants, speaking script, shot mechanics, B-roll/product beats, captions, disclosure/claim considerations, CTA, and QA.
