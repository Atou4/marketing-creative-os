# Creative Typography

Social typography must survive small screens.

- one dominant message per frame;
- clear display/body hierarchy;
- safe margins;
- avoid tiny legal/feature paragraphs baked into images;
- high contrast;
- consistent line breaks;
- keep generated-image text minimal unless the renderer is reliable at text.

Prefer deterministic layout tools for text-heavy carousels and ads.
