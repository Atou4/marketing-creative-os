# Anti AI Slop

## Visual tells
- identical compositions every week;
- generic glossy 3D;
- random neon gradients;
- impossible hands/product details;
- fake UI text;
- over-smoothed people;
- no real brand assets;
- excessive visual polish with no point of view.

## Copy tells
- safe opener + generic middle + generic CTA;
- repeated “not X, but Y” framing;
- empty hype;
- no customer language;
- no specificity;
- manufactured anecdote;
- identical rhythm in every post.

## Fix
Keep humans responsible for insight, taste, truth, and final judgment. Let agents accelerate research, variants, formatting, QA, and repurposing.
