# Native UI Ads

Ads that resemble chats, notes, search, comments, or other interfaces can stop the scroll, but carry deception risk.

Rules:
- do not fabricate real people saying things they did not say;
- do not fake endorsements;
- do not imply the platform itself recommends the product;
- clearly treat dramatized conversations as creative devices when necessary;
- keep product claims traceable.

Use these formats because the communication mechanic fits, not because “fake native” is trendy.
