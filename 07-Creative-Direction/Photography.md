# Photography

Define:
- documentary vs polished;
- people/product balance;
- camera distance;
- crop rules;
- lighting;
- skin tone realism;
- background behavior;
- depth of field;
- prop policy;
- permission/rights.

For human-centered brands, real photography often carries more trust than synthetic perfection. Generated people should not be presented as real customers.
