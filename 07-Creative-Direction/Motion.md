# Motion

Use motion for:
- product behavior;
- state changes;
- visual hierarchy;
- pacing;
- demonstration;
- one memorable transition.

Avoid:
- constant zooms/pans with no reason;
- AI morphing artifacts;
- inconsistent product geometry;
- captions colliding with UI;
- heavy effects that obscure the message.
