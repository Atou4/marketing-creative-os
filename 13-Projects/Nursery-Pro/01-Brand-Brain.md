# Nursery Pro — Brand Brain

## Audience
Primary: nursery owners/directors.  
Secondary: educators and parents.

## Core narratives
- less administrative friction;
- better day-to-day visibility;
- easier family communication;
- smoother educator workflows;
- more organized nursery operations;
- responsible handling of child/family information.

## Voice
Warm, clear, professional, reassuring, human.

## Visual direction
- warm neutrals / cream base;
- strong typography;
- real nursery/educator imagery where licensed/approved;
- real Nursery Pro UI;
- organic shapes used selectively;
- playful details as accents, not the entire identity.

## Avoid
- cartoon overload;
- generic school-template visuals;
- purple AI gradients;
- fake children/parents presented as customers;
- fabricated nursery metrics or testimonials.
