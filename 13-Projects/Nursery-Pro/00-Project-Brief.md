# Nursery Pro — Project Brief

## Product
Nursery management platform for nurseries. Primary buyer: **nursery owner/director**. Secondary audiences: educators and parents.

## Marketing goal
Build a recognizable social presence that communicates operational simplicity, family connection, trust, and modern nursery management—without looking like generic SaaS or childish preschool clip-art.

## Creative thesis
**Warm professional product company with selective childhood warmth.**

## Default approval status
All creative remains draft until human review.
