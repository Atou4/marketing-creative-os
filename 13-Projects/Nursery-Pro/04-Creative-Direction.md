# Nursery Pro — Social Creative Direction

## Ratio
**80% modern/professional product company + 20% childhood warmth.**

## Visual building blocks
- real educator/nursery photography;
- UI crops from Nursery Pro;
- warm cream/neutral surfaces;
- deep readable text color;
- one or two controlled accents;
- organic line/shape annotations;
- editorial compositions;
- large type with simple hierarchy.

## Motion
Short UI demonstrations, subtle annotation motion, light photo-to-product transitions. Avoid hyperactive sticker animation.

## Feed recognition
Keep typography, photo treatment, accent shapes and logo placement consistent; vary layouts and concepts enough that posts do not feel templated.
