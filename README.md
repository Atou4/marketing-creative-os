# Marketing Creative OS

This is a private marketing workspace for planning, writing, reviewing, and learning from creative work. You do not need to be technical to use it.

## Start here

1. Open [[00-Home/Quickstart]].
2. Read [[01-Brand-Brain/MASTER-CONTEXT]].
3. For Nursery Pro, read [[01-Brand-Brain/Nursery-Pro-Brand-Identity]] and then open the project notes in `13-Projects/`.

## The simple workflow

Think of every piece of content as a small journey:

**choose a goal → choose an audience → choose an angle → write a brief → create → review → approve → publish → learn**

The folder keeps the thinking separate from the tools that make images, videos, captions, or posts. A tool can change without losing the strategy.

## Where things live

- `01-Brand-Brain/` — what the brand sounds and looks like;
- `02-Product-Marketing/` — audiences, product truth, objections, and proof;
- `03-Content-Strategy/` — campaigns, pillars, and content planning;
- `04-Hooks-Angles/` — ideas for opening lines and creative directions;
- `05-Formats/` — briefs for posts, reels, carousels, ads, and testimonials;
- `06-Platforms/` — platform-specific guidance;
- `07-Creative-Direction/` — visual rules and review standards;
- `08-Swipe-File/` — references worth studying;
- `09-Agent-Workflows/` — repeatable ways to ask agents for help;
- `10-Agent-Skills/` — specialized creative skills;
- `11-Performance/` — what worked, what did not, and what we learned;
- `12-Research/` — research waiting to be turned into useful knowledge;
- `13-Projects/` — project-specific marketing work;
- `Templates/` — copy-and-use briefs and review checklists.

## What to ask for

You can ask in plain language:

- “Help me plan a launch campaign for Nursery Pro.”
- “Give me three Instagram angles for this feature.”
- “Turn this idea into a short video brief.”
- “Review this ad for brand, claims, readability, and platform fit.”
- “What did our previous creative teach us?”

The system should ask for missing context instead of inventing testimonials, numbers, customer quotes, or product capabilities.

## Approval rule

Drafting and reviewing can be assisted. Publishing, spending money, and making high-risk claims still require a human decision.
