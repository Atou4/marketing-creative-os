# Forbidden Patterns

Record brand-specific things the system should not generate.

## Visual defaults to reject unless intentionally justified
- generic purple/blue AI gradients;
- random glass cards;
- floating 3D blobs;
- fake social screenshots;
- fake chat conversations;
- stock-photo corporate handshake language;
- inconsistent AI faces/brand assets;
- tiny text baked into generated images;
- decorative charts with invented data.

## Copy defaults to reject
- “revolutionize” / “unlock the power” / “seamlessly” used as filler;
- generic contrast framing repeated every post;
- invented founder stories or customer anecdotes;
- fake urgency or scarcity;
- generic “5 tips” with no point of view.
