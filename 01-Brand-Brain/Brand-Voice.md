# Brand Voice

Define behavior, not just adjectives.

## Capture
- What does the brand sound like when confident?
- How does it explain complexity?
- How playful can it be?
- How does it disagree?
- What vocabulary is native to customers?
- Which phrases instantly sound off-brand?

## Voice tests
A draft should pass:
1. **Kitchen-table test** — could a customer say this naturally?
2. **Specificity test** — could this copy belong to ten competitors?
3. **Repeatability test** — can the reader restate the idea after one read?
4. **Truth test** — does every concrete claim exist in the proof bank?
5. **AI-slop test** — remove empty contrast framing, unnecessary hype, repetitive triads, synthetic transitions, and abstract adjectives.
