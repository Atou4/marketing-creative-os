# Nursery Pro — Brand Identity

This page is the quick reference for creating Nursery Pro marketing material. It is based on the current mobile app design tokens and the approved landing-page direction.

## Brand role

Nursery Pro is a nursery-management product for nursery owners and directors. Educators and parents are important secondary audiences.

The product should feel:

- warm and human;
- calm and trustworthy;
- professional enough for daily operations;
- specific to nurseries, not generic school software;
- playful in small, intentional doses.

## Core promise

Make nursery operations easier to run while keeping educators and families connected.

Do not invent customer numbers, testimonials, certifications, or product capabilities. Check `02-Product-Marketing/Proof-Bank.md` before making a strong claim.

## Visual balance

Use approximately 80% modern product company and 20% childhood warmth.

Prefer real product UI, real approved imagery, clear typography, generous whitespace, and a restrained organic shape language. Avoid cartoon-heavy layouts, purple gradients, generic school templates, and AI-looking decoration.

## Typography

- Latin brand type: Quicksand.
- Arabic brand type: Noto Sans Arabic.
- Use bold display type for short headlines and medium-weight body copy for readability.
- Keep body text clear before making it playful.
- Do not introduce a new display font for a single campaign without recording the decision here.

## Imagery

Use the mobile store-listing artwork and approved product screenshots as grounded inputs. Real nursery/educator/child imagery can add warmth, but it must be licensed or explicitly approved. Never present generated or stock people as Nursery Pro customers.

## Character usage

Eggy is a recognizable brand asset, not a mascot that must appear everywhere.

Good uses:

- app icon and store listing;
- launch or onboarding moment;
- small visual cue beside a product explanation;
- campaign artwork with generous negative space.

Avoid:

- repeating Eggy in every section;
- covering product UI with the character;
- cartoon-only ads that hide the nursery-management value;
- changing Eggy’s colors, face, or proportions without approval.
