# Visual Identity

## Stable recognition tokens
- Palette and contrast roles
- Typography hierarchy
- Spacing/rhythm
- Image crop/treatment
- Shape language
- Logo placement and minimum size
- Caption/text-overlay treatment
- Icon style
- Motion behavior

## Controlled variation
Do not freeze the brand into three templates. Keep recognition stable while varying:
- composition;
- visual metaphor;
- crop;
- background;
- content density;
- format;
- camera/shot language.

## Asset truth
Generated visuals must not imply product features, customer endorsements, physical properties, or real events that do not exist.
